# Sources, licenses and presentation assets

- RCSB/wwPDB archive coordinates: public CC0 data; see [RCSB policies](https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies). Retain PDB entry and original scientific publication citations. The curated 3WD5 PDB file is identical to its official download, with SHA-256 in the asset manifest.
- Three.js `0.186.1`: MIT. Locally served unmodified modules and OrbitControls retain their header and [original license](../assets/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt). Exact files, registry source and SHA-256 are in `graphics-vendor.json`.
- Existing Arad font and portfolio assets are reused from this repository, under their existing ownership/terms. The research contribution does not relicense those assets or the rest of the portfolio.
- Generated coordinate preview/contact figures: produced by the project's Python code from the identified experimental coordinates; input hash and method are recorded. The PNG/SVG contact map is a geometric result, not an image copied from a paper.
- `tnf-adalimumab.mp4`: produced by `scripts/animate_binding.py` using ManimGX `0.1.1`; the runtime is not redistributed. Its coordinate source, positive-occupancy selection, engine version, transforms and SHA-256 are recorded. Camera/visibility changes are illustrative, not binding dynamics.
- Articles: only citation metadata and original paraphrases are included. Publisher full texts, article figures and restricted clinical records are not redistributed. Dataset-specific access and licensing are recorded individually; public metadata does not imply unrestricted patient data.
