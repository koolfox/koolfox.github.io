# 7TA3 full-sequence multiconformer feasibility

This CPU-only private Kaggle experiment extends [the single-conformer probe](SEQUENCE-PROBE.md). It constructs the 31-residue sequence, the known Cys2–Cys31 disulfide and the C-terminal amide without using experimental coordinates to generate the conformers. Four independently seeded ETKDGv3 attempts (42–45, one thread, at most 10 embedding iterations and 180 seconds per embedding) are followed by MMFF94 minimization for at most 1,000 iterations. Each subprocess has a 300-second wall-clock limit; the Kaggle job has a 1,500-second platform limit. A seed may fail at installation, embedding, minimization or timeout; a platform COMPLETE alone is not a QC pass. Repeated seed 42 is not a fully controlled convergence comparison with the old run because its iteration budget and environment may differ.

After each attempt, the deposited 7TA3 chain A is used only to compare 31 matched observed Cα atoms by rigid peptide-only least-squares alignment. This measures shape difference from the experimentally bound conformation, not receptor-frame pose or docking RMSD. The coarse checks require converged MMFF, zero intrapeptide heavy-atom pairs under 2 Å separated by more than three covalent bonds, an S–S distance in the diagnostic 1.8–2.3 Å range, and all 31 reference Cα atoms. These chosen checks are not calibrated accuracy thresholds, nor do they guarantee correct stereochemistry, protonation, energies, or conformational sampling. The best of four is selected against the known reference and is optimistically biased; four seeds cannot establish convergence or estimate an equilibrium distribution. The receptor is not included and no binding, docking, affinity, or biological efficacy is evaluated.

The source PDB is the public [7TA3 structure](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7TA3) from [Niu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.1c13717), SHA-256 `3c90d11838c88ebb5501319b367c6ac89e16c67c9a84aa22d17d5a4708052de4`; original retrieval time is unknown. RDKit 2025.9.6 is installed on Kaggle from a version-pinned binary package, but its wheel is not independently hash-verified; the Kaggle image and dependency resolution can change. Only public reference data and code are sent. Kaggle privacy is a service setting.

Build into a new directory using `python3 structural-bioinformatics/kaggle/build_multiconformer.py /tmp/NEW-DIR`; inspect metadata, then submit with `kaggle kernels push -p /tmp/NEW-DIR -t 1500`. Check `kaggle kernels status lakookoo/bioops-7ta3-multiconformer-feasibility` and retrieve outputs with `kaggle kernels output lakookoo/bioops-7ta3-multiconformer-feasibility -p /tmp/NEW-OUTPUT`. Inspect `multiconformer/summary.json` and each seed report, log, and SDF. Outputs also include the public reference; no large local download is needed. The summary is checkpointed after each seed but a platform-level interruption may prevent output publication.
